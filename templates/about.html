About BBA Event Hub

Connecting students through meaningful experiences.

What is BBA Event Hub?

BBA Event Hub is a digital platform designed to help Business Administration students discover upcoming events, activities, competitions, and learning opportunities in one convenient place.

The platform brings together academic activities, student events, business competitions, networking opportunities, and community activities.

Our Mission

To create a simple and accessible platform that connects BBA students with opportunities to learn, participate, connect, and grow.

What You Can Find

📚 Academic Events

Seminars, workshops, and learning opportunities.

🏆 Competitions

Business case competitions, pitch events, and challenges.

🤝 Student Activities

Activities that help students connect and build meaningful experiences.
